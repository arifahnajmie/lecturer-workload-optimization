# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
University lecturers, academic staff administrators and faculty workload coordinators

## What exactly is going wrong?
The distribution of teaching responsibilities may be imbalanced among the lecturers. Therefore, allocating the courses also involve with multiple constraints which includes teaching hours, credit hours, lecturer capacity and course requirements.

## Why does it matter?
It will affect lecturer's well-being because the excessive of teaching workloads. The uneven of allocations also will make it difficult for academic staff to manage it fairly.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
Uneven teaching workload distribution is a challenge in higher education institutions because lecturers may be assigned in different number of courses, teaching hours and credit hours. According to Ariffin et al. (2020), it is a concern about excessive teaching workloads that will affect the lecturers and the importance of fair workload allocation. Therefore, a systematic approach is needed to analyze the workload patterns and support balanced teaching assignments.
